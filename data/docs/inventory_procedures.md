# Inventory Procedures

- Stock counts are recorded every night after closing.
- An item is considered low stock when its quantity is at or below the minimum level.
- Low-stock items must be reordered the same day; the supplier delivers within 48 hours.
- Perishable items (salads, juices, desserts) are ordered in smaller batches, twice a week.
- If an item reaches zero, it must be marked as unavailable on the menu until restocked.

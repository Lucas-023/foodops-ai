# Promotion Policy

- Discounts may not exceed 30% of the regular price.
- Promotions should prioritize products with stock coverage of at least twice the minimum level.
- Never promote products that are at or below minimum stock.
- Combo promotions (main + side + drink) are preferred over single-item discounts.
- Promotions run for at most 3 consecutive days and must be approved by the manager.
- Alcoholic beverages may not be included in "kids" or "family" promotions.
